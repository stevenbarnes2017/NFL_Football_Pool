# Groups

Groups let SundayPickems players compete with their own set of participants.

## Open Groups & Challenges

Open **[Groups & Challenges](/groups)** and select **My Groups**. This page lists the groups available to your account and identifies your active group.

## Creating or Joining a Group

Select **Create** to make a new group, or **Join** to enter a valid group invitation code.

Group administrators can create invitation links for their groups. Once you join, the group becomes available to your account.

## Multiple Groups

Your account may belong to more than one group.

When working with group-specific pages, make sure the correct group is active. Select a group and use **Switch To Group** when necessary.

## Group Standings

Open **[Leaderboard](/leaderboard)** to compare your weekly and season performance with other members of your active group.

Your group's standings are separate from other SundayPickems groups.

## Group Administrators

A group administrator can create invitation links and manage the group. Administrative options appear only when your account has permission to use them.

Group administrators are separate from SundayPickems global administrators.

## Picks and Groups

Always verify that you are working with the intended group when viewing group-specific information.

## Challenges Within a Group

Challenges always belong to a group and can include only active members of that group. Challenge picks and results remain separate from regular pool scoring.

Read **[Challenges](/help/challenges)** for details.

## Membership Help

If you need help joining a group or changing membership, contact your group administrator or [SundayPickems Support](mailto:support@sundaypickems.com).
