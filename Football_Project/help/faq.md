# Frequently Asked Questions

## Where do I make my picks?

Open **Select Picks** from the SundayPickems menu.

## What does a number like -3.5 mean?

The negative number is the favorite's spread.

If a team is **-3.5**, that team must win by at least 4 points for a pick on that team to cover the spread.

## What are confidence points?

Confidence points let you rank how strongly you feel about your weekly selections.

Higher confidence values earn more points when the corresponding pick is correct. Each available confidence value can only be used once during the week.

## Can I change a pick?

Yes, as long as that game's kickoff has not occurred.

Once the game begins, the pick and its confidence selection are locked.

## Why is one of my games locked?

Games lock individually at kickoff.

A Thursday game may already be locked while Sunday's games are still available.

## Do I need to wait until Sunday to make picks?

No. In fact, you should check the schedule earlier in the week because NFL games can be played on Thursday, Saturday, Sunday, Monday, or other days.

## Where can I see how I'm doing during the games?

Open **Live Scores**.

Live Scores lets you follow games and your picks as the action happens. It is especially useful during Sunday's early and late games.

## Where can I review my selections?

Use **See Picks** to review your picks.

## Where can I see the standings?

Open **[Leaderboard](/leaderboard)** to compare results with other participants in your active group.

## Can I belong to multiple groups?

Yes. Open **[Groups & Challenges](/groups)**, select the intended group, and use **Switch To Group** before viewing group-specific information.

## How does the season tiebreaker work?

Predict the total combined score of the designated final game of the season. You can update the prediction from **[Select Picks](/nfl_picks)** until kickoff. If season points and correct-pick totals are tied, the closest valid prediction ranks ahead.

## Why didn't I receive a mobile notification?

Verify that Mobile Device Reminders are enabled and that your browser/device allows notifications from SundayPickems.

See **[Notifications](/help/notifications)** for additional troubleshooting, including the difference between an installed PWA and normal Chrome.

## What are Challenges?

Challenges are short, straight-up winner contests among selected members of an existing group. They remain separate from weekly spread picks and season scoring.

See **[Challenges](/help/challenges)** for more information.

## Where can I find more help?

Open **Settings → Help** to view all SundayPickems help guides.
