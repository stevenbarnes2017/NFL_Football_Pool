# SundayPickems Rules

This guide explains the general rules used for SundayPickems.

## Picking Against the Spread

SundayPickems uses point spreads. The favorite is shown with a negative number.

For example:

**Kansas City Chiefs -3.5**

If you select Kansas City, they must win by at least 4 points to cover the spread.

Your goal is to select the team that will cover the spread for each game.

## Confidence Points

Each weekly pick receives a confidence value.

If a week contains 16 games, the available confidence values are **1 through 16**. Each value may only be used once.

A correct pick earns the confidence points assigned to it. This makes both choosing the correct teams and deciding how confident you are in each selection important.

## Pick Deadlines

Each game locks when that game begins.

Once a game has started, its pick and confidence value cannot be changed. Games later in the week remain available until their individual kickoff times.

Because games can occur before Sunday, always check the week's schedule.

## Scoring

Your weekly score is based on the confidence points earned from correct picks.

Higher-confidence correct picks contribute more points to your weekly score.

Scores and results can be followed using **Live Scores** while games are being played.

## Pushes and Unusual Results

Occasionally, an NFL result may exactly match a point spread or another unusual scoring situation may occur.

SundayPickems applies the scoring rules configured by the site when determining the result. The result displayed by SundayPickems is the result used for pool scoring.

## Missing Spreads

Spreads may not be available immediately when a new NFL week is posted. Review the games again after spreads are available before finalizing your selections.

If a game does not have usable spread information when it is scored, SundayPickems may evaluate it using the available game and winner information configured by the site.

## Weekly and Season Standings

Weekly results contribute to the standings. Open **[Leaderboard](/leaderboard)** to view weekly and season performance for your active group.

## Season Tiebreaker

When a tiebreaker game is available, predict the **total combined score** of the designated final game of the season from **[Select Picks](/nfl_picks)**.

You can update the prediction until that game kicks off. For season ranking, total points are considered first and total correct picks second. If a further tiebreaker is needed, the submitted prediction closest to the game's actual combined score ranks ahead. No prediction loses to a valid submitted prediction at this step.

## Locked or Missing Picks

Players are responsible for submitting and saving their picks before each game's kickoff.

If a game has already started, the selection is locked and cannot be changed.
