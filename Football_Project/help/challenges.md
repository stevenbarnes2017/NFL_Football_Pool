# Challenges

Challenges are short, separate contests between members of an existing SundayPickems group. They do not change your normal weekly picks, confidence points, weekly score, or season leaderboard.

The current Challenge format is simple: **pick the straight-up winner of each selected game**. The participant with the most correct selections wins, and ties are allowed.

## Find Your Challenges

Open **[Groups & Challenges](/groups?section=challenges)** and select the **Challenges** section.

You can see a Challenge when you are its creator or a participant. Group administrators and SundayPickems global administrators can also view Challenges they manage.

## Create a Challenge

Any active member of a group can create a Challenge for that group:

1. Open **[Groups & Challenges](/groups?section=challenges)**.
2. Make sure the intended group is your active group.
3. Select **Create Challenge**.
4. Enter a name and optional description.
5. Select at least one additional active group member.
6. Select one or more available games from the current NFL week.
7. Select **Create Challenge** to finish.

The creator is included automatically. Selected members become participants immediately; there is no invitation or acceptance step in the current version.

## Make Challenge Picks

Open the Challenge and select either the home or away team for each game, then select **Save Picks**.

Challenge picks are straight-up selections. Spreads and confidence values are not used.

You can change a selection while its game is open. Every game locks independently at its scheduled kickoff, so a Thursday selection may lock while Sunday and Monday selections remain editable.

## When Picks Become Visible

Before a game kicks off, you can see your own selection but not another participant's selection or whether they have submitted one.

At kickoff, all submitted selections for that game become visible. Later games remain hidden until their own kickoff times.

If someone did not select a team, **No pick submitted** appears after that game locks.

## Standings and Winners

Challenge standings show each participant's correct, graded, and remaining games.

- Standings are provisional while games remain unfinished.
- An NFL tied game is graded but does not award a correct selection to either team.
- A Challenge is completed only after every selected game has a final result.
- Everyone tied for the highest correct count is a winner.

Results are calculated from the current NFL game scores. If a score is corrected, the Challenge standings update automatically.

## Cancelled Challenges

The creator, an active administrator of the Challenge's group, or a SundayPickems global administrator may cancel an open or in-progress Challenge. A completed Challenge cannot be cancelled.

Cancellation does not delete the Challenge. Its participants, games, submitted picks, and available history remain visible, but no picks can be added or changed.

## Regular Picks Are Separate

Challenge selections never replace or update your regular pool selections. Continue using **[Select Picks](/nfl_picks)** for weekly spread and confidence picks.

For regular pool instructions, read **[How to Make Your Picks](/help/making-picks)**.
