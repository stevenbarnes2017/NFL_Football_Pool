# How to Make Your Picks

Welcome to SundayPickems! Each week, you'll pick the NFL teams you think will beat the spread and rank your picks using confidence points.

## 1. Open Select Picks

After signing in, open **[Select Picks](/nfl_picks)**. The current NFL week will normally be selected automatically. You can use the week selector to view another week when available.

## 2. Review the Games

Games are grouped by the day they are played. Each matchup shows the teams and the point spread for the favorite.

For example:

**Kansas City Chiefs -3.5**

The negative number is the favorite's point spread.

## 3. Pick a Team

Select the team you think will cover the spread.

If Kansas City is **-3.5**, Kansas City is favored by 3.5 points. If you pick Kansas City, they must win by **4 points or more** for that pick to be correct.

The larger the negative number, the more points the favorite must win by to cover the spread.

## 4. Assign Confidence Points

Assign a confidence value to each pick. Each confidence number can only be used once during the week.

Put your highest confidence value on the pick you feel strongest about.

If there are 16 games, confidence values will range from **1 through 16**. A correct 16-point pick earns more than a correct 1-point pick.

## 5. Save Your Picks

Select **Save Picks** when finished. Make sure your picks successfully save before leaving the page.

You can return later and change picks that have not locked.

## 6. When Picks Lock

Each game's pick locks when that game begins. Once it starts, you can no longer change that game's pick or confidence selection.

Games that have not started remain available.

**Don't wait until Sunday.** NFL weeks can include Thursday, Saturday, Sunday, Monday, and other games.

## 7. Follow Your Picks Live

Once games begin, open **Live Scores** to follow the action throughout the day.

Live Scores is especially useful on Sundays when several games are being played at once. You can check game results and follow how your picks are doing as scores are updated.

For more information, see **[Live Scores & Following Your Picks](/help/live-scores)**.

## 8. Review Your Picks

Use **[See Picks](/see_picks)** to review your selections. Open **[Leaderboard](/leaderboard)** to compare your results with other members of your active group.

## Quick Checklist

- Pick a team for every game.
- Assign each confidence number only once.
- Put your highest confidence points on your strongest picks.
- Save your picks.
- Check for games that start before Sunday.
- Use **Live Scores** once the games begin.
