# Live Scores & Following Your Picks

**Live Scores** lets you follow NFL games and your SundayPickems activity while games are being played.

It is especially useful on Sundays when several games may be happening at the same time.

## Opening Live Scores

Open **[Live Scores](/live-scores)** while games are in progress.

The page displays the current week's games and available scoring information.

## Following Games

As NFL games are played, SundayPickems updates game scores and results.

You can use Live Scores instead of manually checking each matchup and then trying to calculate how your picks are doing.

## Following Your Picks

Live Scores helps you see how the teams you selected are performing against their spreads.

Remember that SundayPickems is based on the spread, so simply seeing your selected team winning the NFL game does not always mean that pick is currently covering.

For example, if your team is **-7.5**, it needs to be ahead by more than 7.5 points to cover the spread at that moment.

## Sunday Game Days

Live Scores is designed to be particularly useful during the main Sunday NFL schedule.

Check it throughout the afternoon as early and late games progress, and continue using it for Sunday night and other games during the week.

## Scores Can Change

Games in progress are not final. A pick that is covering in the third quarter may not cover when the game ends.

Final SundayPickems scoring is based on the final game result.

## After the Games

Use **[See Picks](/see_picks)** to review your selections and **[Leaderboard](/leaderboard)** to see completed standings.

For information about scoring and confidence points, see **[SundayPickems Rules](/help/rules)**.
