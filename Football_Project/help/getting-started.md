# Getting Started with SundayPickems

New to SundayPickems? This guide covers the basic workflow.

## 1. Sign In

Sign in to your SundayPickems account.

## 2. Join or Select Your Group

SundayPickems allows players to participate through groups. Make sure you are viewing the group you want to play in.

If you belong to multiple groups, open **[Groups & Challenges](/groups)**, choose the group, and select **Switch To Group** before using group-specific pages.

## 3. Select Your Picks

Open **Select Picks**.

Choose your selection for each game and assign your confidence points.

For detailed instructions, see **[How to Make Your Picks](/help/making-picks)**.

## 4. Save Your Picks

Save your selections before leaving the page.

Games lock individually when they begin, so check for games scheduled before Sunday.

## 5. Follow the Games

Once games start, open **Live Scores**.

This is the best place to follow the week's games and see how things are progressing throughout game day.

## 6. Review Results

Use **[See Picks](/see_picks)** to review your selections and **[Leaderboard](/leaderboard)** to compare your results with other players in your group.

## 7. Come Back Each Week

Repeat the process for each NFL week:

**Select Picks → Save Picks → Live Scores → See Picks & Leaderboard**

## More Help

Visit **Settings → Help** for [rules](/help/rules), [groups](/help/groups), [challenges](/help/challenges), [notifications](/help/notifications), and [frequently asked questions](/help/faq).
