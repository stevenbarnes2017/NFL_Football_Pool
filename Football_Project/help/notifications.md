# Notifications

SundayPickems can send reminders and updates to supported mobile devices and browsers.

Notifications are optional and can be controlled by each user.

## Mobile Device Reminders

Open **Settings → Profile** and find **Mobile Device Reminders**.

Enable **Remind me on my mobile device for week-start reminders**, then select **Save Notification Settings**.

## Allow Browser Notifications

Your browser or installed SundayPickems app may ask for permission to send notifications.

Choose **Allow** if you want to receive them.

Both your SundayPickems notification setting and your device/browser permission must be enabled.

## What Notifications Are For

SundayPickems may use notifications for useful pool updates, such as reminders that help you prepare your picks or let you know when information needed for picking is available.

## Not Receiving Notifications?

Check the following:

- Mobile Device Reminders are enabled in SundayPickems.
- Notifications are allowed for SundayPickems in your browser or device settings.
- You are using the same device/browser where notifications were enabled.
- Your device is connected to the internet.
- Battery-saving or notification-silencing settings are not delaying SundayPickems alerts.

## Installed App and Browser Notifications

An installed SundayPickems PWA and a normal Chrome browser window can use different notification permissions and subscriptions, even on the same phone.

Enable notifications from the version you normally use. If you switch between the installed app and Chrome, verify permissions in both places.

On Android, review the relevant app or Chrome site settings under **Settings → Notifications**. The exact menu name varies by Android version and device manufacturer.

If permission was previously denied, your browser may not ask again automatically. Re-enable SundayPickems notifications in the browser's site settings, then return to **Settings → Profile**.

## Turning Notifications Off

You can disable SundayPickems notifications through **Settings → Profile**.

You can also manage site notification permission through your browser or mobile device.

## Still Having Problems?

If your settings appear correct but notifications are not arriving, note whether you use the installed app or normal Chrome and contact [SundayPickems Support](mailto:support@sundaypickems.com).
